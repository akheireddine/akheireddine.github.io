# Description 
I am a research engineer in the R&D team of [DOWSERS](https://www.dowsers.finance/). Before that, I was a Temporary research and teaching Assistant (ATER, Attachée Temporaire d'Enseignement et de Recherche) in computer science at Sorbonne Université.  Prior to that, I was a PhD student in the [LRE](https://www.lre.epita.fr/) team of EPITA and MoVe team of [LIP6](https://www.lip6.fr/) laboratories of computer sicence, under the supervision of Étienne Renault and Souheib Baarir.

My current work focuses on the verification of smart contracts on the Solidity blockchain using formal verification techniques.

I am affiliated with the [LIP6](https://www.lip6.fr/) laboratory (MoVe team) and continue to teach at Sorbonne university in addition to my work at DOWSERS.
